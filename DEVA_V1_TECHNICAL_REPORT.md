# DEVA v1.0 technical report

This release incorporates the evidence-backed [Phase-5 research report](RESEARCH_REPORT_PHASE5.md) unchanged. Its experimental results, source identities, negative ablation findings and scope are the scientific report of record.

## Classification of statements

Observation: recorded solver outputs and synthetic sensor measurements, with provenance.
Experimental result: frozen held-out failure counts, fixed-design cost trade-offs and exploratory search comparisons. These do not establish universal reliability or algorithm superiority.
Interpretation: two domain adapters operated through the same registered-tool investigator support bounded architectural generality, not universal engineering intelligence.
Formal result: Boolean topology and rational necessary-energy abstractions checked with Z3, under explicitly recorded assumptions. They do not prove physical hydraulic or electrical reality.
Limitations: field calibration and rupture validation absent; steady hydraulic research equations; perfect-foresight finite-horizon energy adequacy without AC dynamics; software-rendering performance limitations.
Future work: FUTURE_RESEARCH.md. These research extensions do not expand frozen v1.0 scope.

Release qualification is tracked in V1_RELEASE_GATES.md and the release evidence package. Until actual deployment and public smoke pass this remains a release candidate, not DEVA v1.0 complete.
