# DEVA v1.0

Two engineering worlds share a bounded registered-tool autonomous investigator, candidate ancestry, Pareto search, counterexamples, uncertainty and domain-scoped evidence. Water includes pressure-dependent hydraulic service and synthetic calibration; microgrid includes chronological storage, conversion losses and energy-adequacy dispatch. Formal results are property-specific topology/energy abstractions.

Historical negative findings and budget trade-offs remain visible. See V1_SCOPE.md, RESEARCH_REPORT_PHASE5.md and REPLAY_MANIFEST.json. The validated Phase-5 baseline is 119 Python, 15 frontend, 8 browser tests and seven exact replay checks; current release validation is recorded separately rather than assumed.

Public container deployment and public smoke gates pass at https://deva-vert.vercel.app. Responsive 2D is supported; software 3D is slow and WebGPU acceleration is unestablished. Field calibration and rupture prediction remain unvalidated; microgrid does not model AC dynamics.
