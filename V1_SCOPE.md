# DEVA v1.0 — scope freeze

DEVA is an autonomous engineering research laboratory demonstrating multi-domain design exploration, numerical simulation, adversarial testing, optimisation, bounded verification, uncertainty-aware evidence and reproducible investigation.

Frozen domains: water infrastructure and microgrid energy adequacy. Python supplies scientific truth; strict TypeScript supplies interaction; WebGL and numerical 2D consume computed state. Registered tools, bounded search, domain-scoped evidence and stored-data demonstrations are included.

DEVA is not a certified design tool, professional-engineer replacement, real-time infrastructure controller, field-certified hydraulic platform, full AC simulator or universally optimal solver. No third domain, new physics engine or research extension belongs in this release.

Historical results remain permanent: 38/200; 44/200 → 13/200 at increased cost; 4/40 hospital failures (2% target unestablished); fresh 22→7→5/120 trade-off with +8.47 units for the known intermediate budget relaxation, not a global minimum. Water/microgrid frozen populations fail 93/512 and 300/512. Larger searches fail 16/120 and 31/120. Thirty ablation trials did not establish general guided-search superiority; random search performed better on the recorded microgrid study.

Release blockers: broken workflows, altered evidence, unsupported claims, unsafe public computation, failed required tests, missing deployment or public smoke testing. Research limitations: field calibration, calibrated rupture, AC dynamics, arbitrary topology synthesis, rare-event guarantees, publication-grade comparisons and hardware WebGPU. These do not block a accurately bounded research release.
